# ADR: MVP Taxonomy Freeze

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-09-20

The approved `consolidated_taxonomy_expansion_plan.csv` is applied only through a versioned runtime artifact, `mvp-taxonomy-v4.1-frozen`; the original Reference Taxonomy workbook remains immutable.

The MVP may use the approved aliases, `财务专员`, and `技术服务工程师` (including only its approved domain links). It must not execute P2/P3 review, automatically expand taxonomy coverage, or promote pending/generic titles. Unknown titles retain their raw evidence and remain eligible for Job Grouping. Reopening taxonomy requires a demonstrated systemic taxonomy bug and explicit approval.
