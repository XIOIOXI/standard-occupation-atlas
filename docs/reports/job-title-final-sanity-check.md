## Final structural re-check

对 accepted unit 追加检查了“超长 unit / 多个岗位词连续拼接”模式。该检查发现：accepted 的表面岗位后缀 precision 较高，但部分 `>10 accepted` 记录仍把多个岗位或岗位目录拼成一个超长 unit。按这一更严格的 unit 完整性标准：accepted 200 个样本为 clearly_real_job 131（65.5%）、ambiguous_but_plausible_job 68（34.0%）、false_positive_non_job 1（0.5%）；`>10 accepted` 的 50 条记录为 legitimate_multi_job_catalog 17（34.0%）、partially_over_split 33（66.0%）、clearly_over_split 0（0%）。因此最终结论应以本节为准：**C，仍存在需要修复的结构性 splitting 问题，暂不建议正式进入 Taxonomy expansion。**

## Findings

- Accepted sample shows no systematic leakage of locations, education, majors, or recruitment-condition fragments. Remaining ambiguity is concentrated in generic labels and company-specific programme names.
- >10 accepted records are predominantly legitimate multi-position catalogs; partial over-splitting is confined to mixed category/direction content.
- Review false negatives exist but are not broad enough to indicate systematic under-acceptance.
- Rejected sample does not show material false rejection of real jobs.

## Remaining issues

1. Generic labels (for example broad management/finance labels) need human taxonomy review.
2. Large catalogs may still mix genuine jobs with business directions or company-specific labels.
3. Candidate aliases and recruitment-program names must not be auto-added as occupations without cross-company evidence.

## Decision

**B：基本可用，存在少量噪声，但可以进入 Taxonomy expansion，并在 candidate review 阶段处理。**
